Everything your phone app does, on your Mac

AirBuds only shows the controls your earbuds say they support. On a model without ANC, the noise control section simply doesn't appear.

Battery and time left

Left bud, right bud and case, an estimate of time left, and a chart of your listening session.

Noise control

Noise Cancellation, Standard or Transparent in one click, with the current mode shown in the menu bar.

EQ presets and Custom EQ

Normal, Rock, Pop, Classical, Jazz and Country, or shape 10 bands from 31 Hz to 16 kHz yourself.

Touch controls

Choose what single, double and triple tap do on each bud. Saved on the earbuds.

Per-app automations

Transparent while FaceTime is open, Low Latency for a game. Switches back when the app quits.

⌃⌥N

Keyboard shortcut and links

Switch the noise mode from any app, or use airbuds:// links from Shortcuts and scripts.

Battery alerts

A notification at 20% and again at 10%, and when a bud or the case is fully charged.

Volume and Low Latency

A volume slider and mute for the earbuds, and game mode for tighter audio sync in games and video.

Connect, rename, device info

Connect and disconnect without Bluetooth settings, rename your earbuds, and see the firmware version.

Feels at home on macOS

Native Swift and SwiftUI, in light and dark mode, with your system accent color.

Install in a minute

  1. 1

    Download and drag to Applications

    Get AirBuds-{{VERSION}}.dmg, open it and drag AirBuds into Applications.

  2. 2

    Allow it to open

    AirBuds isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks the first launch. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway, or run: 

    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/AirBuds.app"
  3. 3

    Allow Bluetooth and connect your earbuds

    Click Allow when macOS asks for Bluetooth access. Pair your earbuds in System Settings as usual, then click the earbuds icon in the menu bar.
